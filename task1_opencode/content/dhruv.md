## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI assistants are great for boilerplate, refactoring and explaining unfamiliar code, which saves a lot of time. But they still make confident mistakes, so every suggestion needs a careful review. I treat AI as a fast junior colleague, not as a replacement for understanding the code.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
Developers will spend less time typing code and more time designing systems, defining requirements and reviewing results. Cloud and infrastructure work will be increasingly automated, so architecture and reliability skills will matter even more.

## My hot take on AI
AI won't replace developers, but developers who ignore it will fall behind those who use it well.
