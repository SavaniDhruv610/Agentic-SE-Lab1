## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI tools help me learn faster by explaining algorithms and suggesting alternative solutions. The risk is that beginners skip the struggle that builds real problem-solving skills. It is most valuable when I try first and use AI to check and improve my work.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
Writing code will become cheap and abundant, so the hard part will be deciding what is worth building. Engineers will work more like technical product owners, guiding AI agents and verifying their output.

## My hot take on AI
Code is getting cheap; knowing what to build and why is the skill that stays expensive.
