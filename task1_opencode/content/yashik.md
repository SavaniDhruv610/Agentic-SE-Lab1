## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI is very good at generating UI components and quick prototypes, which speeds up the first draft considerably. The weak spot is judgment: it does not know our users, so design decisions and accessibility still need a human. Used blindly, it also produces code nobody on the team fully understands.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
Prototypes will go from idea to working demo in hours, and non-programmers will build small tools themselves. Professional engineers will be valued for product thinking, quality and maintainability rather than raw coding speed.

## My hot take on AI
Prompting is the new Stack Overflow: useful until you stop understanding the answer.
