## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI helps with writing tests, summarizing data and exploring ideas quickly. Its output still depends on the quality of the question and the data, and it can sound right while being wrong. I find it most useful when I validate its results against real data and tests.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
Quality assurance will shift from manual checking to designing strategies that verify AI-written software at scale. Engineers who understand data, testing and user needs will be in high demand.

## My hot take on AI
AI writes the code fast; tests decide whether anyone should trust it.
